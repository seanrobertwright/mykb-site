mykb privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

mykb is a personal tool used by its developer and members of their household to search their own data. Each person connects their own Google account and consents to read-only access. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it accesses. With the user's consent, read-only access to their own Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Tasks. It never sends, changes, or deletes anything.
  • Where data goes. The data is stored only on the developer's home computer, in one database that the household members who use that computer can search. Long items (such as long email threads) are sent to the Anthropic API to produce search summaries. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and, by default, does not use API data to train models. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • Removal. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local database removes all stored data.
  • Google user data. Use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
